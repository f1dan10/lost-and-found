# User journey

This journey is by Tamar, a junior student at MIT.

**The loss.** On Monday Tamar has a recitation in Building 2, lunch in the Stud (W20), a lecture in Stata, and a lab in Building 34. On her way into the lab she notices that her pink water bottle is gone. She has no idea which of the three buildings it is in, and retracing her route would make her late. She could send a dormspam email or call the desks one by one, but neither that is a lot of work.

**Step 1: she checks one place.** Tamar opens Lost&Found and clicks "Found" (Sketch 1). She scrolls through the cards looking for a bottle, then opens the Map (Sketch 2) to check the green pins on Stata, W20, 34, 2. Her bottle is not there yet.

**Step 2: she posts what she lost.** She clicks the plus button and chooses "Add Lost Item". She picks the category "Bottle", writes "pink water bottle", and selects the four buildings from the dropdown. Her report now shows as a red pin on each of them, and she goes to her lab.

**Step 3: someone finds it.** An hour later Tom, a student she has never met, sees a pink bottle in Building 2. He would not carry it across campus to MIT Police, but he does not have to. He clicks the plus button, chooses "Add Found Item", takes one photo, picks "Bottle" and "Stata", and keeps walking. The bottle stays on the bench.

**Step 4: she is told.** Tom's report is a bottle in one of the buildings Tamar listed, so she gets an email with a link to his card. The photo shows her bottle.

**Step 5: she claims it.** Tamar clicks "This is mine" on the card. The claim is recorded under her MIT account, and Tom gets an email saying who claimed it.

**The outcome.** After lab Tamar walks to Stata and picks up her bottle. The found report is closed and its pin leaves the map. She posted once and made one trip to the right building, and Tom spent less than a minute.
