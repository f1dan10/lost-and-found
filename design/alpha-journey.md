# Alpha user journey

Tamar, a junior at MIT, has a recitation in Building 2, lunch in the Stud (W20), a lecture in Stata, and a lab in Building 34. On her way into the lab she notices that her pink water bottle is gone, and she has no idea which of the four buildings it is in. She opens Lost&Found, signs up with her MIT username, and checks the Found list and the Board, which shows a tile for each building. Nothing has been found yet. She clicks the plus button, chooses "Add Lost Item", picks "Bottle", writes "pink water bottle", and selects the four buildings, and then she goes to her lab. An hour later Tom, a student she has never met, sees a pink bottle in Building 2. He would not carry it across campus to MIT Police, but he does not have to. He signs up, clicks the plus button, chooses "Add Found Item", takes one photo, picks "Bottle" and "Building 2", and keeps walking. The bottle stays where it was. Tom's report is a bottle in one of the buildings Tamar listed, so a notice appears on her bell within a few seconds, and it is also sent as an email, which the Alpha prints to the server's console. She clicks it and gets redirected to Tom's card, where the photo shows her bottle. She clicks "Claim" and confirms. The report closes and its card leaves the Found list, and Tom's bell now says his item was claimed by tamar. After lab Tamar walks to Building 2 and picks up her bottle. She posted once and made one trip to the right building, and Tom spent less than a minute.
