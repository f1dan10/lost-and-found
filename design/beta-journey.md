# Beta user journey

Tamar, a junior at MIT, has a recitation in Building 2, lunch in the Stud (W20), a lecture in Stata, and a lab in Building 34. On her way into the lab she notices that her pink water bottle is gone, and she has no idea which of the four buildings it is in. She opens Lost&Found on her laptop, signs up with her MIT username, and searches "bottle". Nothing has been found yet. She clicks "Report an item", chooses "I lost something", picks the Bottle chip, writes "pink water bottle", types "2", "stata", "34" and "w20" into the building box to add the four buildings, and posts it. Then she goes to her lab. An hour later Tom, a student she has never met, sees a pink bottle in Building 2. He would not carry it across campus to MIT Police, but he does not have to. He reports it as found, picks Building 2, writes "by the lobby bench", adds one photo, and keeps walking. The bottle stays where it was. Tom's report is a bottle in one of Tamar's buildings, so her bell lights up within a few seconds, and "Waiting on you" in her sidebar shows it under "Possible matches". She opens it, sees her bottle in the photo, and clicks "This is mine". The panel asks whether it really is hers, because Tom will see her username, and she confirms. The panel tells her where to pick it up, Building 2 by the lobby bench, and offers to close her lost report, which she does. After lab she walks to Building 2, takes the bottle, and clicks "I picked it up". The claim moves to her History, and Tom is told that his bottle reached its owner. She says thanks with a quick note, "You saved my day!", and Tom reads it with his report. She posted once and made one trip to the right building, and Tom spent less than a minute.
