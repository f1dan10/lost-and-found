# P5 flaws and opportunities

| # | Flaw | Why it happens | Proposed improvement | Level | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Map and search show one item under several buildings.** Participant 1 searched "Media Lab", saw markers on other buildings too, and found only one item actually there (P1). | A lost report lists every building it may be in, so it appears under each. Nothing on the screen says so. | Mark lost items as "Maybe in 3 buildings" with the others listed, and use a distinct marker for lost and found. | Conceptual | Moderate. They recovered once it was explained, but the facilitator had to explain. |
| 2 | **Buildings box is not recognized as a multi-select field.** Participant 2 did not see that it can be typed into or that several buildings can be added (P2). | It looks like a plain text box and gives no cue until a building is chosen. | Label "Add buildings (up to 12)", show the list on focus, and add a hint such as "Not sure where? Add every place it could be." | Physical | Moderate |
| 3 | **The notification is easy to miss and its reason is opaque.** Participant 2 did not notice the bell and asked how matching works (P2). | The bell is small, and the notice does not say why the item matched. | Add a banner on the Lost list and a count on "Waiting on you". State the reason: "Same category and shared building: Stata". | Linguistic | Moderate. Participant 1 saw it at once, so it depends on the user. |
| 4 | **The Returned section is unclear.** Participant 2 did not understand it (P2). | The name suggests "returned to me", but it shows all recent claims on campus. | Rename to "Recently reunited" with a one-line explanation under the title. | Linguistic | Minor |
| 5 | **Required and optional fields are not marked.** Participant 2 could not tell which fields were needed (P2). | The form marks the photo "optional" but nothing else. | Mark every field as "required" or "optional" the same way. | Linguistic | Minor |

## Further ideas from the sessions

- **Outdoor places** (P1): add a few places such as "Outdoors, Killian Court" or "On the street".
- **Password recovery** (P2): not built; MIT sign-in would solve it.
- **Private groups** (P2), such as a dorm: worth considering but a large change, as it alters who can see reports.
